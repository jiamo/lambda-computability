# M28-STARHEIGHT-S4-COXETER and M28-STARHEIGHT-LIFT

## Results (Lean, no sorry)

* `GeneralizedStarHeight.S4Cox.height_one_S4_coxeter` (`Start/StarHeightS4Coxeter.lean`): for
  `coxT := FreeMonoid.lift ![swap 0 1, swap 1 2, swap 2 3] : FreeMonoid (Fin 3) →* Perm (Fin 4)`
  and every `F`, `HasHeightAtMost {w | coxT (ofList w) ∈ F} 1`.
* `GeneralizedStarHeight.height_one_of_lift` (`Start/StarHeightLift.lean`), the R2 statement:
  `π.comp S = T`, `E` finite, every coset language `{w | S w ∈ H i * {g}}` of height ≤ 1 and
  `⨅ i, H i ≤ π.ker` give height ≤ 1 for every `{w | T w ∈ F}`.  Proof: equal cosets for all
  `i` force `S u (S v)⁻¹ ∈ ⨅ H i ≤ ker π`, so `T u = T v`; then `height_one_of_determined`.
* `GeneralizedStarHeight.height_one_of_determined`: observers of *words* `ob i : List α → X i`
  with finite `X i`, finitely many `i`, height-one fibres, and `(∀ i, ob i u = ob i v) → T u = T v`
  give height ≤ 1 (the language is a finite union of finite intersections of fibres).
  Finiteness of the `X i` is needed: with `ob = id` the hypotheses hold for any `T`.

R1 is an instance of R2 with the group `E` below, but the Lean proof uses the word-level form
`height_one_of_determined` directly, with the four observers of the certificate.

`N` is the aperiodicity index used in `Aperiodic.certOK` (`f^[N+1] = f^[N]` for every element of

count (`height_one_of_comm`).

Determination (`coxT_eq_dec`): `coxT w i = dec (A1 w) (A2 w) (P w) (par w) i` for all `w`, `i`,
by induction on `w` (append one letter).  All `4·4·3·2 = 96` observation tuples occur, so the
decoding table `decL` is total; `dec_step` (`decide +kernel`, 96·3·4 cases) checks
`dec (A1 a x) (A2 b x) (P p x) (e+1) i = dec a b p e (swt x i)`, and `dec_start` the empty word.
Permutations are handled as `Fin 4` tables only; no `decide` on `Perm`.  Lean's convention
`(σ * τ) i = σ (τ i)` is used throughout (the letters are involutions, so the other convention
gives the inverse and the same determination).

## The group E and how the family was found

`E` = the group generated by the signed permutations of `{±0, ±1, ±2, ±3}` sending `i ↦ j`,
`j ↦ -i` for the letter `(i j)`: `|E| = 192`, `ker π` of order 8.  Search script
`docs/goal/evidence/M28-STARHEIGHT-LIFT-SEARCH.py coxeter` (exact enumeration of all subgroups
of `E`, as joins of cyclic subgroups of prime-power order): `E` has 12 proper good subgroups (13
with `E`): eight of index 4 and two of index 6 with star-free first returns, the index-3 pairing
subgroup (`P`), and the index-2 kernel of the sign (star-free and abelian).  Their intersection
has order 2 and lies in `ker π`.  Smallest families meeting inside `ker π`: size 3 (two index-4
automata and one index-6 automaton); the certificate above uses A1, A2 (index 4), P (index 3) and
the parity (index 2).  No single good automaton of `E` together with the good coset automata of
`S₄` determines the element (re-checked: with the good subgroups containing `ker π` it leaves an
intersection of order 32 or 48 outside `ker π`), which is why the single-automaton search failed.
