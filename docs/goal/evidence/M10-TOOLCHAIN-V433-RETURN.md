## Sources brought forward to the current APIs

* `Start/OracleMachine.lean`, `Start/Inseparable.lean`, `Start/OracleSim.lean` — `Nat.rfind`
  and `Nat.rfindOpt` no longer rewrite under `simp`/`rw` at the `ℕ →. Bool` type, so the
  least-witness characterisations go through `Nat.mem_rfind.mp` and `Nat.mem_rfind.mpr`
  (whose second component now takes `m` implicitly) and through an explicit unfolding of
  `Nat.rfindOpt` as an `rfind` followed by a `bind`.
* `Start/OracleSim.lean` — `RecursiveIn` is now the bundled `Primcodable` predicate and
  `Nat.RecursiveIn` the numeric inductive one.  The closure rules
  `Lambda.Oracle.recIn_zero`, `recIn_succ`, `recIn_left`, `recIn_right`, `recIn_pair`,
  `recIn_comp`, `recIn_prec` and `recIn_rfind` transport each constructor across
  `RecursiveIn.iff_nat`, and are used throughout `Start/OracleSound.lean`,
  `Start/OracleUniversal.lean`, `Start/OracleJoin.lean` and `Start/OracleJump.lean`.
* `Start/OracleSound.lean`, `Start/OracleUniversal.lean`, `Start/OracleJump.lean` — the
  `Part`-monad bookkeeping around those closure rules is now explicit (`Part.bind_map`,
  `Part.bind_some`, `Part.bind_eq_bind`), and the two `rfind` characterisations of `Φ_e^A`
  are proved from `Nat.mem_rfind` instead of by `simp`.
* `Start/Representation.lean` — `cslib`'s full β-reduction is the congruence closure
  `Xi Beta` of the root rule, so the case analyses use `Xi.base (Beta.beta …)`, `Xi.appL`,
  `Xi.appR`, `Xi.abs`; `Term.LcAt` is `Bool`-valued; `Term.subst_intro` no longer takes a
  local-closure argument; confluence is `Term.confluent_fullBeta`.
* `Start/TM2Forward.lean`, `Start/TM2Capstone.lean` — `Turing.Reaches`, `Turing.eval` and
  `Turing.mem_eval` are now in the `StateTransition` namespace.
* `Start/TM2Partrec.lean`, `Start/TM2PolyTime.lean` — `Computability.FinEncoding` is
  deprecated in favour of a bare encoding function together with a `Fintype` alphabet.
* `Start/CwaType.lean` — morphisms of `Type u` are bundled, so pointwise equality of
  morphisms goes through `ConcreteCategory.hom_ext`.
* `Start/LambdaPiInitial.lean` — the substitution square is closed by two private
  `eqToHom` lemmas, since the goal is no longer type-correct at implicit transparency.
* Deprecation and linter follow-ups: `Mathlib.Data.Nat.Lattice` →
  `Mathlib.Order.Lattice.Nat`, `Set.mem_setOf_eq` → `Set.mem_ofPred_eq`, `Set.mem_setOf` →
  `Set.mem_ofPred`, `push_neg` → `push Not`, `haveI` → `have` on a propositional goal,
  removal of three `beta_reduce` calls that do nothing and of one unused `simp` argument.
  `Cwa.Model` carries a scoped `set_option linter.checkUnivs false in`, exactly as Mathlib
  does for its own bundled categories: its three universes are independent but occur only
  inside a `max`.
