# Lift searches: S4 over three letters (R3), universal S4, F20, K3 (R4)

Computational evidence, not Lean-verified, except where a Lean theorem is named.  Script:
`docs/goal/evidence/M28-STARHEIGHT-LIFT-SEARCH.py` (subcommands quoted below).  Lift test: for
auxiliary letters `L x` in a permutation group `K`, `E = <(T x, L x)> ≤ G × K`; a subgroup of `E`
of index ≤ 12 is good if its coset automaton has star-free first returns or `E/H` is abelian; a
hit means the good subgroups meet inside `ker π`.  Subgroups of `E` are enumerated exactly (all
subgroups); lifts with `|E| > 800` are skipped and counted.  Classes are written as image tuples
`p0 p1 p2 p3` in the right-action convention (`p * q` = first `p`, then `q`).  Both the hit set
and the open set below are closed under inverting all three letters, so they are the same in
Lean's convention (Lean letters = inverses).
